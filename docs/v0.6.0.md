# Studium v0.6.0: the plan

Scope set by the operator on 2026-11-13 (`caspian-kb/rules/sources/2026-11-13T1907Z-session-f063313e.md`): "lo
cosmetico dejemos para la 0.6.0, donde tambien agregaremos grupos de trabajo, amigos, Spotify y YTMusic, y mas cosas",
plus, by his answers the same night, feedback and proposals, a game for deciding, and the sound session.

**How it is reviewed.** Every pull request deploys its own preview by itself since 0.5.3 (its address is commented on
the pull request), and the work is iterated there: "con lo cosmetico primero me vas a mostrar paginas temporales
desplegadas automaticamente en Firebase antes de mergear un PR ... e iremos iterando para que no danes mas cosas en
el camino". A preview reads and writes the live database. What needs new database rules (groups, friends, feedback)
is proven on the local preview (emulators) until the merge publishes the rules.

## 1. Lag, measured first

"La pagina sigue un poco lageada, mas lageada para que me sienta comodo interactuando con ella."

- Measure before touching anything, on his laptop and on a phone-class CPU (4x slowdown): frames per second while
  scrolling the landing, the home and the map; long tasks; time to interactive; memory over five minutes.
- Fix what the numbers point at. Then measure again and write both sets of numbers in the pull request.
- Done means: scrolling holds 60 frames per second on the laptop and does not stutter on the slowed profile.

## 2. The landing

His words, item by item:
- **Too crowded.** The main problem. A person who just arrived is overwhelmed by the amount of text.
- **«guías, apuntes y datos de cada profe, hechos por estudiantes»** goes away: the paragraph below says the same.
- **«psst: lo que flota se puede lanzar. Las letras también»** stays, further down.
- **«CMP-4005»** goes away from the public page: USFQ codes are shown only to signed-in students.
- **«Gratis, no vendo nada jaja»** must be read, not lost small among everything else.
- **«No la mía: no tengo tantos servidores ni plata…»** must be legible, by its size and its place.
- **The explanatory animations must represent what they say.** The «AK» square dragged in «IA integrada, personalizada
  para ti» goes away ("YO NO DEBERIA ESTAR AHI, ES UNA SECCION SOBRE IA, NO SOBRE MI"). Every scene is checked for
  whether it shows its own idea.
- **More depth:** 3D, gradients, objects that do not look flat.

It follows the Webber persona (`caspian-kb/roles/webber/`) and his verdicts in `TASTE.md`. It keeps the accepted
notebook identity: blue rules, red margin, highlighter gradients, depth and play, blue and red as the main colors,
and no purple.

## 3. Friends and study groups: by invitation, without chat (his choice)

- **Friends.** A friend is added with a link or a code. Each person chooses the name their friends see. Nobody else
  sees it, and nothing they contribute shows it.
- **Groups.**
  - A group is a room with a code.
  - It has a shared focus timer and shared music (below).
  - It shows who is studying now, and fixed, positive reactions.
  - There is no free text, as in the rest of Studium.
- **Rules.** New database rules cover friends, groups and their members, with tests on the emulators.

## 4. Music: YouTube in full, Spotify embedded (his choice)

- **YouTube.**
  - A player inside Studium, with its own controls, on the YouTube IFrame Player API.
  - It plays YouTube and YouTube Music links (songs and playlists).
  - In a group it plays in sync for everyone: one person leads, and the rest follow play, pause, seek and the track.
- **Spotify.**
  - Its embedded player, for the playlist, album or track the person pastes.
  - Full tracks when the person is signed in to Spotify in that browser; previews otherwise.
  - Not synchronized in groups.
- **Why not more.** Spotify's Web API admits only a few hand-approved users for a small app, and its Jams have no
  public API.

## 5. Feedback and proposals

- Any member sends a comment or proposes a feature.
- It reaches only the admins, who read and answer it from the admin panel.
- The sender sees the answer.

## 6. A game for deciding

- For when you do not know what to choose: what to study first, whose turn it is.
- Inspired by the idea of ConchShell, with a design of its own.
- It has the sound and motion of Studium.

## 7. The sound session

- A session dedicated only to the audio, with him: "ahora los sonidos son decentes (califico en un 5/10), pero estan
  lejos de la perfeccion y requieren una sesion dedicada SOLO al audio".
- His standing verdicts in `TASTE.md` apply: quiet by default, dynamic, never repeated the same way, glass and deep,
  never a bell on every hover.

## Order

1. Lag, measured and fixed.
2. The landing, iterated on the preview.
3. Friends and groups.
4. Music.
5. Feedback and the game.
6. The sound session, when he can sit for it.
7. One pull request `v0.6.0` when everything is complete and proven.
