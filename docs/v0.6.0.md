cosmetico dejemos para la 0.6.0, donde tambien agregaremos grupos de trabajo, amigos, Spotify y YTMusic, y mas cosas",
plus, by his answers the same night, feedback and proposals, a game for deciding, and the sound session.

Revised 2026-11-19 with what he sent Squire from 11-16 to 11-18 (`caspian-kb/agenda/inbox/`, his words in
`rules/sources/`). From those messages, 0.6.0 takes only what is part of what it already builds:
- the schedule shared with friends and «¿cuándo podemos todos?» (`2026-11-16T205412Z`, `2026-11-16T223513Z` [05:56]);
- the contact section, in his tone (`2026-11-18T192833Z`), which is the front door of feedback;
- preset avatars (`2026-11-16T221205Z` [02:10]), because friends and groups show people.

Everything else, and why it waits, is in `roadmap.md`. The urgent schedule builder ships before this, as 0.5.4
(`v0.5.4.md`).

**How it is reviewed.**
- Every pull request deploys its own preview by itself since 0.5.3; its address is commented on the pull request. The
  work is iterated there: "con lo cosmetico primero me vas a mostrar paginas temporales desplegadas automaticamente en
  Firebase antes de mergear un PR ... e iremos iterando para que no danes mas cosas en el camino".
- The pull request is opened as a draft with the first commit, so that a preview exists from the first day and not only
  at the end.
- A preview reads and writes the live database. Whatever needs new rules (friends, groups, feedback, shared schedules)
  is proven on the local preview (emulators) until the merge publishes the rules. On the PR preview those parts say in
  one line that they open after the merge.

## 1. Lag, measured first

"La pagina sigue un poco lageada, mas lageada para que me sienta comodo interactuando con ella."

- **Measure before touching anything.**
  - Where: on his laptop, and on a phone-class CPU (4x slowdown).
  - What: frames per second while scrolling the landing, the home and the map; long tasks; time to interactive;
    memory over five minutes.
- **Fix what the numbers point at.** Then measure again, and write both sets of numbers in the pull request.
- **Done means:** scrolling holds 60 frames per second on the laptop and does not stutter on the slowed profile.
- **It comes first** because everything after it (groups, music, live presence) adds work to the page. Each later step
  is measured again against these numbers.

## 3. Friends, and the people you see

- **A friend is added with a link or a code.**
  - The code is long and random, and the person can make a new one, which voids the old one.
  - Friendship needs both sides: the link proposes it, and the other person accepts it.
  - Either side ends it alone.
- **Your name for friends.** Each person chooses the name their friends see; `nickname` in the profile today is reused.
  Nobody else sees it, and nothing they contribute shows it.
- **Preset avatars.**
  - A set drawn for Studium, in the notebook style, with depth.
  - They replace the six colors of today's `avatar` field, which become their backgrounds.
  - No uploaded pictures: they cost storage and need moderation. Uploads and a Duolingo-like builder are in the roadmap.
- **Only members of the same community** can be friends, as with everything else in Studium.

## 4. Study groups: by invitation, without chat (his choice)

- **What a group is.**
  - A room with a code: the same kind of code as friends, renewable by its creator.
  - The creator can remove a member.
- **What it has.**
  - A shared focus timer, the same one of the home.
  - Shared music (below).
  - Who is studying now.
  - Fixed, positive reactions.
- **There is no free text in a group**, as in the rest of Studium.
- **"Who is studying now"** follows the rule of `presence/{uid}`: it is written on real events (joining, starting the
  timer, leaving), never on a timer.
- **Reads stay in the free plan.**
  - A group is one document plus one per member, listened to only while the group is open.
  - The budget is written in the pull request: reads for a group of six over a two-hour session.

## 5. Your schedule with friends, and «¿cuándo podemos todos?»

His words: "en vez de hacer los polls en Whatsapp" (`2026-11-16T205412Z`), and "deberías poder compartir este horario con
tus amigos si es que tú lo quisieras" (`2026-11-16T223513Z` [06:27]).

- **Sharing is optional.** The schedule of 0.5.4 stays private by default. A person may share it with chosen friends
  or with a group: only the busy hours, or the classes too.
- **«¿Cuándo podemos todos?»**
  - In a group, or with chosen friends, it shows the hours when all of them are free this week.
  - It is computed in the browser, deterministically, from the shared schedules: "sería más fácil hacerlo con un modelo
    determinista".
  - A proposed hour is fixed with one tap, and it lands in each person's «Fechas y entregas».
- **No AI is needed for it.** The person's own AI may later suggest, as he said ("la IA podría apoyar ahí
  opcionalmente"); that waits for 0.7.0 with the rest of planning.

## 6. Music: YouTube in full, Spotify embedded (his choice)

- **YouTube.**
  - A player inside Studium, with its own controls, on the YouTube IFrame Player API (no key is needed).
  - It plays YouTube and YouTube Music links, songs and playlists, pasted by the person. No search: search needs the
    Data API and its daily quota.
  - In a group it plays in sync for everyone: one person leads, and the rest follow play, pause, seek and the track.
  - The browser allows sound only after a tap. A follower presses «Escuchar con el grupo» once.
  - A video whose owner forbids embedding says so in words and offers to open it on YouTube.
- **Spotify.**
  - Its embedded player, for the playlist, album or track the person pastes.
  - Full tracks when the person is signed in to Spotify in that browser, previews otherwise. This is checked on the
    preview with a free account and a Premium one before it is written on the page.
  - Not synchronized in groups.
- **Why not more.** Spotify's Web API admits only a few hand-approved users for a small app, and its Jams have no
  public API. So "compartir jams de Spotify" is honestly not possible from Studium. The group shares YouTube instead.

## 7. Feedback, proposals and «Escríbeme»

- **Anyone signed in writes.** A member sends a comment, reports an error or proposes a feature.
  - It is the first free text in Studium. Only the admins and its sender read it, so it opens nothing to anyone else.
  - Its fields: kind (error, idea, comment, "porque sí"), the text, and the page it came from, with the version and the
    browser for an error.
  - No screenshots. They would need file storage, which Studium does not have; a link to one is accepted in the text.
- **The admins answer** from the admin panel, and the sender sees the answer in «Escríbeme».
- **A summary on demand.** In the panel, the admin's own AI (the ways of `ai.js`) can summarize what arrived and group
  repeated errors. It runs on demand only, as everything in the panel: never on a timer.
- **The contact section** (`2026-11-18T192833Z`), in his tone, half playful, half rebellious:
  - why to write: an error, an idea, a suggestion;
  - and a reason that is not formal: "o escríbeme porque sí".
  - The joke about being single, which he asked to keep on record: proposed copy is shown to him on the preview.
    Nothing personal is published without his yes.
  - It is only for signed-in members, as the rest of what is about him beyond the landing.
- **Rules:** `feedback/{id}` is written by its sender. It is read by its sender and the admins, answered only by the
  admins. It has limits of size and of rate (a few per day per account).

## 8. A game for deciding

- **What it is for:** for when you do not know what to choose, such as what to study first, or whose turn it is.
- **Its own idea.** He named the app ConchShell. Its idea comes from the Magic Conch of SpongeBob, which is someone
  else's character. Studium takes the idea (ask, and something decides for you), not the shell, its phrases or its
  voice.
- **A design of its own, from the notebook.**
  - A paper fortune teller (comecocos), folded from a notebook page.
  - A coin and a die drawn in the margin.
  - «¿A quién le toca?»: in a group, it picks among the members who are there.
- **It has the sound and motion of Studium.** Its sounds are made in the sound session.

## 9. The sound session

- **A session dedicated only to the audio, with him:** "ahora los sonidos son decentes (califico en un 5/10), pero estan
  lejos de la perfeccion y requieren una sesion dedicada SOLO al audio".
- **His standing verdicts in `TASTE.md` apply:** quiet by default, dynamic, never repeated the same way, glass and deep,

- **It covers the new sounds too:** reactions, joining a group, the timer of a group, the game.
- **It does not hold the release.** If he cannot sit for it before everything else is proven, 0.6.0 ships with the
  current sounds, and the session becomes 0.6.1.
