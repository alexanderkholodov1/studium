# Studium v0.5.0: the plan

Scope set by the operator on 2026-11-05, after using v0.4.4 live. Three headline changes: an admin system with a
detailed panel, a page that no longer lags, and sound redone from scratch. Everything else below goes in the same
version.

## 1. Sound, redone

What is wrong in v0.4.4:

- **Hover is far too much.** Every panel the pointer crosses rings like a bell. It must be almost imperceptible:
  low and deep, more felt than heard.
- **Sounds repeat without end.** Keeping the pointer on the same object, or dragging Nodo, keeps triggering sounds.
  Find the cause in the event handling and in the collisions while dragging, and fix it.
- **Sounds have no dynamics.** They switch on and off, and every one is the same sound at another pitch. That is
  what makes them stressful.

What v0.5.0 needs:

- Continuous sounds with envelopes that follow what the person does. Each gesture gets its own sound: touch, press,
  release, drag (friction that follows the speed), move, throw (air scaled by the release velocity), impact (by
  mass and speed), shake.
- Rich variation: the partials, filters, noise and envelopes are randomized per hit, not only the pitch. Use modal
  and granular techniques where they fit.
- Low loudness by default, with quiet limits per category (hover, interface, physics, celebrations), and a mix
  check on laptop speakers and on headphones.
- The same standard for every sound on the page: buttons, toggles, page changes, goals, the timer and the radio.

## 2. Performance

- The page lags on a computer and worse on a phone. Profile it before changing anything. Likely causes:
  - backdrop-filter and the liquid SVG filters on many glass panels;
  - the animation loop that runs all the time for the tokens;
  - large blurred glows that animate;
  - the 3D sheets.
- On the phone, the background orbs follow the screen while scrolling, which makes no sense. Fix that, and lighten
  or turn off the heavy effects on small screens.
- Targets: smooth scrolling (about 60 fps) on a mid-range laptop and a mid-range phone. Measure before and after,
  in the browser's Performance panel and with Lighthouse.

## 3. Playful ambience

- The bubbles look flat: make them look 3D, like glass, or remove them.
- They come far too often, and popping bubbles over every page only gets in the way. Keep them in a few thematic
  places, or behind a personal setting that is off by default.

## 4. Accounts and communities

- **Email confirmation off.** Microsoft 365 holds the confirmation e-mails, so the requirement only gets in the way.
  It becomes an admin switch that starts off. The rules read the same switch.
- **USFQ alumni addresses accepted.** The domain is `alumni.usfq.edu.ec`; its MX record points to Microsoft 365, as
  `estud.usfq.edu.ec` does.
- **Communities managed from the admin panel**: each with a name, its accepted domains and whether it is active. USFQ
  becomes one community among others. The configuration moves from `config.js` into a settings document that the
  rules also read.

## 5. Admin system

- `studiumplatform@gmail.com` is an admin, through a custom claim (`admin: true`) set with the admin API.
  `isAdmin()` in the rules. An admin can sign in even though the address belongs to no community.
- **An admin panel at `#/admin`, very detailed and with full controls.**
  - Settings: email confirmation on or off; accepted domains per community; communities (create, edit, turn off);
    global flags (hints, playful bubbles, radio, which AI providers are offered); an announcement banner.
  - Users:
    - how many are registered, and how many are active now and over the last 1, 7 and 30 days;
    - a list with e-mail, community, created, last sign-in, confirmed, admin;
    - disable, enable, delete, make or remove admin.
  - Content: counts of guides, contributions, documents, professor tips and thanks; find, hide or delete any item.
  - Firebase usage:
    - Firestore storage size;
    - reads, writes and deletes today against the free quota (50,000 / 20,000 / 20,000 per day);
    - Authentication users;
    - hosting transfer.
  - Easter eggs: the full list, with how each is triggered, and how many people found each one (anonymous counts).
  - E-mail: the sender in use, a test send, and the state of the e-mail links.
  - The deployed version, the release, the rules version and the last deploy.
  - An audit log of every admin action.
- **How the data reaches the panel.**
  - Counts that the rules allow an admin to read come straight from Firestore.
  - What needs admin credentials (the user list, usage metrics from Cloud Monitoring) comes from a scheduled job:
    a GitHub Action, free, runs every hour and on demand, and writes `admin/stats` and `admin/users`.
  - User actions (disable, delete, claims) go through the same job, or through a small server if they must be
    instant. Decide while building.
  - Active users: a light presence heartbeat, written at most every few minutes, so reads and writes stay within the
    free quota.

## 6. Your AI

- Several providers saved at once, one key each.
- Model and effort are chosen when the AI is used, not fixed in Settings. Not every task needs the strongest model.
  - Each task recommends a model: a strong one to create a guide, a light one to identify a document.
  - The last choice is remembered per task.

## 7. Interface fixes

- The profile menu (the avatar) is too transparent and its text blends into the background: make it opaque and
  legible.
- «Fuentes y agradecimientos» do not belong under the profile or in the footer. They go to the Map, as a light
  credits button.
- The Map also needs an info button that explains why a knowledge graph helps.
- Hints:
  - drop the obvious ones;
  - give them a proper entrance and exit animation (today they vanish without one);
  - place them next to what they talk about, not always in the bottom-left corner.

## 8. Landing texts, each in its own section (the design stays)

The landing page is fine as it is. What fails is the copy written for it, which has no soul and does not sound like
the operator. The corrections are for the sections he named. Use his own phrases, and propose any new text to him
before publishing it.

- **The first section (the hero).** «Tu espacio para estudiar lo que sea. Y una comunidad que se pasa los apuntes.»
  makes no sense and has no soul. In its place go his texts, which today sit in the manifesto section where they
  do not belong:
  - «Hecho por estudiantes, para estudiantes. Gratis, no vendo nada jaja»
  - «Guías de estudio, material de clases, ejercicios, exámenes de práctica, proyectos finales. Úsalo para tus clases
    o tus hobbies, y aporta al crecimiento de la comunidad de Studium.»
  The page's meta description repeats the old line; change it too.
- **The manifesto section** (shared by the landing and «Acerca»). The paragraph about professors must not be
  public: «Con quién tomar Redes. Quién da puntos extra. Qué profe toma asistencia…». That knowledge stays inside,
  for members.
- **The «IA integrada, personalizada para ti» section.** Its drawing has only a light in the middle and makes no
  sense: put «IA», or something clear, at its center.
- **The section of the note that becomes the graph.** The sample note (a name, «Nota del parcial: 17/20») has the
  opposite effect: it looks as if Studium collects that data. The drawing must show the person's own notes being
  turned into anonymized information that feeds the knowledge graph.
- **The «Dos espacios, una sola cuenta» section** is confusing, because Studium does not really have that.

## 9. E-mail (open)

- Firebase does not let this project change its e-mail template or where the links point
  (`EMAIL_TEMPLATE_UPDATE_NOT_ALLOWED`). Studium already handles the links itself (live-tested in v0.4.4).
- With confirmation off, this matters only for password reset. Two options:
  - our own mail through Google Apps Script in the Studium Gmail account, free, up to 100 a day;
  - the Blaze plan.
