a session of its own.

## 1. Your AI, in several ways

A person can connect any of these, as many as they like. The page then proposes, for each use, **where** to run it,
which model and which effort, among what that person has, and says why in one line.

| Way | What the person needs | Automatic | Cost to them |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| API key (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) | A key from the provider's console | Yes | What the key uses |
| Gemini, free key | Any Google account; a key from AI Studio in a minute, no card | Yes | Nothing, within Google's daily limits; on the free tier Google may use what it receives |
| Local bridge | Node, and Claude Code, Codex or Antigravity signed in on their computer | Yes | Their subscription |
| OpenRouter | An OpenRouter account; one click, no key to copy | Yes | Nothing on free models (50 requests a day, 1,000 with credits bought once) |
| Puter | A Puter account; one click | Yes | Puter's own allowance |
| Chat | Any account, free Claude included | By hand: copy, attach, paste back | Their account |

- **Errors in words.** "Your credit balance is too low" says that a claude.ai subscription does not include the API,
  and offers the other ways. A daily limit says when it resets. A provider that refuses a key stops only its own
  requests.
- **What each way reads.** A way that cannot read PDFs gets their pages as pictures, as scans already do.
- **The bridge** is one file the person downloads from Studium and runs with `node`. It listens only on this computer,
  answers only Studium's page, and only after it is paired with a code that it prints. It runs the person's own CLI,
  read-only, in a folder of its own that it deletes afterwards. Gemini CLI is no longer offered to personal Google
  accounts (it sends them to Antigravity), so Antigravity takes its place.

## 2. A queue for any amount of material

- **Drop anything:** files, folders, ZIPs, hundreds at once. Each part waits in the queue.
- **It survives.** The queue and its files live in this browser (IndexedDB), so a reload, a closed tab or a crash
  resumes where it stopped. When the browser has no room for the files, the queue keeps its place and asks for the
  same folder again, matching each file by its fingerprint.
- **One tab works at a time** (Web Locks); the others show its progress.
- **Limits are waited out, never fought.** A rate limit waits as long as the provider says. A daily limit pauses that
  way until its reset and resumes alone. Network and server errors retry with growing waits. An account problem stops
  that way and offers another.
- **Progress:** parts done of the total, what is running, what waits and why.
- **Chat parts** sit in the same queue: copy the instructions, download the part, paste the answer back.
- **Publishing in bulk** (his choice): every result waits for review, and «Publicar todo lo listo» publishes at once
  those with nothing personal to check. Those with a warning wait for the person.
- Duplicates by fingerprint are skipped, as today.

## 3. Proof

- **Automatic tests** of the queue and the bridge: a reload in the middle, a rate limit, a daily limit, an account
  without balance, two tabs, the same file twice, a bridge asked from another origin or without its code.
- **Validation as a user**, with small batches of the Draconic Knowledge Hub through the page itself on the local
  preview (emulators), never by hand. The rest of the hub is his test set, after his merge, in production and in
  batches.
- Checks green, then the PR `v0.5.3`.
